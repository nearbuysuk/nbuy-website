## 1. Overview

Welcome to **nBuy** ("we," "our," or "us"). nBuy provides a location-based mobile discovery and reservation platform that connects local brick-and-mortar retailers and service providers ("Retailers") with consumers searching for nearby products and services ("Consumers").

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is shared between Consumers and Retailers when a reservation is placed, and the choices you have regarding your data.

> **Development Status Notice:** The nBuy mobile application is currently in active development and pilot testing. This policy governs data collected through our website, pilot onboarding forms, and pre-release mobile applications.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Consumer Information
- **Location Data (Foreground Only):** When you search the nBuy map for nearby products or services, we process your approximate or precise device location (with your explicit operating system permission) solely to display nearby Retailers, distances, and local inventory availability. We do **not** track your background location when the app is closed.
- **Reservation Details:** When you reserve a product or service, we collect your name, contact details (email address and/or phone number), and the items or service time slots requested so the Retailer can hold your reservation.
- **Device & Diagnostic Data:** Basic crash logs and performance metrics used to ensure compatibility and reliability across mobile devices.

### 2.2 Retailer Information
- **Storefront & Business Profile:** Business name, physical store address, geographic coordinates (for map pin placement), operating hours, public phone number, and store category.
- **Inventory & Service Listings:** Product titles, descriptions, pricing, stock availability indicators, and bookable service descriptions uploaded or synced to nBuy.
- **Merchant Account Contacts:** Name, business email, and billing or administrative contact details for authorized store personnel.

---

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect strictly to operate and improve the nBuy service:
1. **Local Map Discovery:** Displaying live store pins and in-stock products or services within a Consumer's selected search radius.
2. **Reservation Fulfillment:** Transmitting reservation requests from a Consumer to the selected Retailer so the item or service slot can be held in-store.
3. **Transactional Notifications:** Sending confirmation alerts, hold-expiration reminders, or cancellation notices related to an active reservation.
4. **Platform Security & Fraud Prevention:** Preventing automated scraping, spam reservations, or abuse of Retailer inventory holds.

---

## 4. How Information Is Shared

- **Between Consumers and Retailers:** When a Consumer submits a reservation, the selected Retailer receives the Consumer's name, reservation reference code, and requested item/service details solely to fulfill that in-store reservation.
- **No Sale of Personal Data:** nBuy does **not** sell Consumer or Retailer personal information or location history to third-party data brokers or ad networks.
- **Infrastructure Providers:** We use trusted hosting, map tile, and transactional notification providers bound by strict data processing agreements.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Your Rights

- **Location Queries:** Map search coordinates are processed ephemerally to return search results and are not linked to persistent movement profiles.
- **Access, Correction & Deletion:** Both Consumers and Retailers may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their account data at any time by contacting us at `legal@ourtech.app`.
