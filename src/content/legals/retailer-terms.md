## 1. Scope of Retailer Participation

These Retailer & Merchant Terms ("Retailer Terms") supplement the general nBuy Terms of Service and govern brick-and-mortar stores, local boutiques, and service providers ("Retailers") that list products or services on the nBuy Platform.

---

## 2. Storefront Eligibility & Verification

To list inventory or services on nBuy's location discovery map, a Retailer must:
1. Operate a verifiable physical retail location or local service premises open to consumers during stated operating hours.
2. Provide accurate geographic coordinates, street address, and contact information so consumers can reliably visit the store.
3. Have full legal authority to offer and sell the goods or services listed on its store profile.

---

## 3. Inventory Listing & Prohibited Items

### 3.1 Good-Faith Availability
Retailers control which products, quantities, and service slots are visible on nBuy. You agree to use reasonable efforts to keep listed stock statuses accurate and promptly pause or delist items that sell out on the shop floor.

### 3.2 Prohibited Goods & Services
Retailers may not list:
- Counterfeit, stolen, or unauthorized replica goods;
- Items whose sale or possession violates local, state, or national laws;
- Hazardous, age-restricted, or regulated items unless compliant in-person age and identity verification is performed at the counter upon pickup.

---

## 4. Handling Consumer Reservations

1. **Timely Confirmation:** When a Consumer submits a reservation request during your active store hours, you agree to confirm or decline the hold promptly within the nBuy merchant interface.
2. **Honoring Confirmed Holds:** Once you confirm a reservation, you agree to set the reserved item aside (or hold the confirmed service slot) until the stated Hold Expiration Time at the price displayed at the time of reservation.
3. **In-Store Checkout & Consumer Rights:** The Retailer is the seller of record for all in-store transactions resulting from an nBuy reservation and remains responsible for collecting applicable sales taxes/VAT, issuing receipts, and honoring statutory consumer warranty and return obligations.

---

## 5. Brand Assets & Map Display

By creating a store profile on nBuy, you grant nBuy a non-exclusive, royalty-free license while your account is active to display your store name, logo, physical location pin, store hours, and uploaded product/service listings within the nBuy mobile app and promotional materials.

---

## 6. Pilot Program & Termination

Retailers participating in the nBuy pre-release pilot may pause their storefront visibility or terminate participation at any time by notifying `partners@ourtech.app` or toggling their store status offline in the merchant portal.
